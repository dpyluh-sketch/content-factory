content-factory

A private publishing tool for one YouTube channel.

What it does

content-factory uploads short videos to its owner's own YouTube channel and manages them: it sets the title, description, category, visibility and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure, and reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time) to see how the videos perform.

It is used by a single person, the owner of the channel, on their own computer. It is not a public service: other people cannot sign up, and it does not access anyone else's channel.

How it uses Google data

The app signs in with Google OAuth and uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only for the owner's channel. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
