content-factory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

content-factory is a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the channel uses the app, on their own computer. It does not collect data from viewers or from any other YouTube users, and it does not access other channels.

What YouTube data the app uses

  • Basic channel information of the signed-in account (channel ID, title) — to know which channel to upload to.
  • The channel's own videos and their metadata (title, description, category, visibility, synthetic-content disclosure, thumbnails, playlists, captions) — to upload and edit them.
  • Comments on the channel's own videos — to read and reply to them.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources) — to see how the videos perform.

How the data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in the macOS Keychain. Video files and statistics stay on the same computer. The app has no server and no database of its own.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer YouTube or Google user data to anyone. The data is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access at any time on the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions → content-factory → Remove access. After that the app can no longer read or change the channel. Locally stored tokens can be deleted from the macOS Keychain.

Data deletion

To delete locally stored data, remove the app's entries from the macOS Keychain and its local files. Videos already published on YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio.
